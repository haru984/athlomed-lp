About this document

This consent has two parts, because they are given at different times.

PartWhenWhat you consent toHow it is recorded
Part AWhen you create your account (once)Storing your records in AthlomedSaved in your consent history
Part BEach time you issue a connection codeShowing your records to one specific team, within a range you chooseSaved with each code, together with the language you used and the version of the consent text

This split is the heart of this document.
Creating an account does not make your records visible to anyone. They become visible only when you give the Part B consent and issue a connection code.

A-1 What Athlomed is

Athlomed is a condition and injury management platform for sports settings.

From physical checks through condition, injury, rehabilitation and return to play.
Bringing the records of the field together into one.

Athlomed is built to handle data that may count as medical information as sparingly as possible, while still providing the recording functions a sports setting needs. For you, that means:

  • Your records belong to you, not to a team. If you change teams or graduate, your records stay with you.
  • You decide who sees them. A trainer cannot register you without your involvement.
  • You can narrow what you show, later. Showing something once does not mean showing it forever.
  • Coaches never see your diagnosis or your treatment.

A-2 Athlomed is not a medical institution

Athlomed is not a medical institution and is not for diagnosing, treating or preventing illness or injury. What is recorded here does not replace a doctor's diagnosis. If something is wrong with your body, see a medical professional.

The alerts and figures on screen are material to think with, not a judgement in themselves.

Of these, injury and rehabilitation records and photographs of the affected area may count as "special care-required personal information" under Japanese law — information that must be handled with particular care. That is exactly why we ask, each time, who may see it (Part B).

A-4 Nothing is visible to anyone until you share it

Once you have created an account, you are the only person who can see your records. You can record your condition, and those records are stored as yours.

They become visible to a trainer or coach only when you issue a connection code and a trainer uses it.

A-6 You cannot see clinical note bodies

The body of the clinical note (SOAP) your trainer writes is not displayed to you.

To be direct about why: clinical notes sometimes contain provisional impressions and reasoning about other possibilities that have not been confirmed. Read as they stand, these cause anxiety and misunderstanding. And if notes are written on the assumption that you will read them, trainers write less, and the record itself gets worse.

This is not about hiding information from you. If you want to read them, there is a written procedure (a disclosure request). Contact us at the address at the end of this document.

A-8 If you are a minor

If you are 18 or over

You can register on your own consent.

If you are under 18 — a guardian must consent

After you complete the consent below, you will be asked to enter the email address of your guardian (a parent or other legal representative). A confirmation email goes to that address; once your guardian has reviewed it and consented, your records can begin.

① You consent
      ↓
② You enter your guardian's email address
      ↓
③ A confirmation email is sent to that address (valid for 7 days)
      ↓
④ Your guardian reviews the document and consents
      ↓
⑤ You can record data and issue connection codes

Until your guardian consents, you cannot do the following.

Before guardian consentAfter guardian consent
Log inYesYes
Record condition and similar dataNoYes
Issue a connection codeNoYes
Viewing by team staffNoYes (within scope)

Enter your guardian's own email address. Do not enter another address of your own, or a friend's address. This step exists to protect you: consent obtained through the wrong address is not valid consent.

If the link expires after 7 days, you can request a new one from your screen.

What your guardian will review is set out in the Guardian Consent guardian_consent.

A-9 If you want to stop

You can stop at any time. Withdrawing a required item means closing your account. Optional items can be withdrawn individually.

Nothing bad will happen to you because you withdrew.

However, information that has already been seen cannot be unseen.

B-1 What you are consenting to right now

You are about to issue a six-digit connection code. You give this code to a trainer; when they enter it, the team they are currently logged into can see your records.

This consent covers this one connection. To connect with a different team, you will be asked again.

B-2 Choose how much to show

OptionWhat becomes visibleWhen to choose it
From today onward only (recommended, default)Only records made after you connect. Nothing from beforeJoining a team or transferring, normally
Including injury historyThe above, plus your past injury records and return progress (not clinical note bodies)When you want a new trainer to know about a previous injury
Full historyEverything, including past clinical notesWhen the same trainer will keep following you at a different team

If you are unsure, choose "From today onward only." You can widen it later.

B-3 What to know about the code

  • The code is shown once, when you issue it. Athlomed does not store the code itself, so it cannot be looked up later. If you lose it, issue a new one.
  • You choose how long it lasts (30 minutes or 24 hours). After that it stops working.
  • It works once. No second team can connect with the same code.
  • You can cancel it at any time before it is used.
  • Check who you are giving it to. Hand it over in person, spoken or by QR code.

B-4 What happens after you connect

  • You receive a notification: "You are now connected to [team]." You will never be connected without knowing.
  • That team's trainers can see records within the range you chose, and can enter clinical notes and measurements.
  • That team's coaches cannot see your diagnosis or your treatment. They see the injured body part, how far you may train today, and condition trends.
  • No other team can see anything.

B-5 If you want to stop or change it

What you wantHowWhen it takes effect
Show lessChange the range on the "Connection code" screenImmediately. What you removed disappears from the trainer's screen
Show moreSameImmediately
Disconnect entirelyRelease the connection on the "Connection code" screenImmediately. That team can no longer see anything
  • Disconnecting does not delete your records. They remain yours and you can still see them. If you connect again, they become visible according to the range you choose then.
  • What has already been seen cannot be unseen. Also, clinical notes written by a trainer remain with that trainer after you disconnect. If you want them deleted, submit a request to the contact at the end of this document.
  • Nothing bad will happen to you because you stopped.

B-6 Please also note

  • Athlomed is not a medical institution. Its records are not diagnosis or treatment.
  • You cannot see clinical note bodies (the reason is in A-6).
  • Connections being formed and released are recorded in the audit log.
  • If information needs to go outside Athlomed — to a hospital, for example — you will be asked separately each time. Consenting here does not send anything outside.
  • If you are under 18, a guardian (legal representative) must consent.
