Why you received this email

Your child is trying to register for a service called Athlomed.

Athlomed records and manages condition and injury information in sports settings. Because your child is under 18, no records can begin without a guardian's consent.

Your child entered this email address themselves as the contact for that consent. That is why you are receiving this.

If this is unexpected

If you were not expecting this email, do not open the link and do not consent.

Your child may have entered this address by mistake, or a third party may have entered your address. In either case, no records begin unless consent is given. The link stops working after 7 days.

We would be grateful if you would let us know at the contact address below, so that we can look into it.

Chapter 1 What to know first

1.1 Athlomed is not a medical institution

Athlomed is not a medical institution and is not for diagnosing, treating or preventing illness or injury. What is recorded does not replace a doctor's diagnosis. If you have concerns about your child's health, see a medical professional.

Alerts and figures on screen are material for judgement in the field, not medical judgement in themselves.

1.2 The records belong to your child

In Athlomed, records attach to your child, not to a team. They continue across changes of team and across graduation.

And who may see them is decided by your child (and you). A trainer cannot register your child unilaterally: a connection is formed only when your child issues a six-digit code and a trainer receives it.

Chapter 2 What guardians can do

2.1 There is no viewing function for guardians

Athlomed has no function for a guardian to log in and view a child's records, and no guardian account.

This follows from Athlomed's design, in which the athlete (and guardian) decide, case by case, who may see the records. A standing view for guardians would undercut that premise.

2.2 What you can do

Stop your child using the Service (withdraw consent)The contact in Chapter 5, or withdraw the account

2.3 How this email address will be used

The address you consent with will be used only to:

  1. confirm this consent and send you a copy of what you consented to;
  2. notify you when your child connects with a team;
  3. inform you of revisions to this and related documents;
  4. respond to enquiries from you.

It will not be used for advertising or promotion, and will not be provided to third parties.

Note on implementation: Item 2 (notification to guardians when a connection is formed) is not implemented in the Service as at the date of this document. Until it is, this communication takes place through the team's trainer.

D. Information sharing

Keyguardian_d_team_sharing
TypeRequired
CoversSharing with authorised staff of a connected team

Who it is shared with, and how much

  • Sharing reaches only the staff of a team to which your child gave a connection code. No other team sees anything.
  • Coaches do not receive the diagnosis, how the injury happened, whether surgery was performed, clinical note bodies or photographs of the affected area (1.4).

Not covered by this item

  • Disclosure outside Athlomed — to a medical institution or anyone else — is not covered. As set out in E below, that requires separate consent each time.
  • Sharing with third parties who are not part of the team is not covered.

D-1. How consent to each connection will be given

Each time your child connects to a team, a consent is required deciding "which team, and how much to show" (Athlete Consent, Part B). That consent is given by your child themselves.

Chapter 4 If you do not consent

4.1 You may decline

No detriment will follow for your child from a refusal to consent. It will not affect how they are treated within the team, their playing time or selection.

4.2 Nothing is recorded until you consent

Your child has already given their own consent, but until your consent is complete they cannot do the following.

Before guardian consentAfter guardian consent
Log inYesYes
Record condition and similar dataNoYes
Issue a connection codeNoYes
Viewing by team staffNoYes (within scope)

4.3 Link expiry

The confirmation link expires 7 days after it is issued. If it expires, your child can request a new one from their screen.

4.4 If you decline any of A to D

A to D are indispensable to using Athlomed. If any of them is declined, your child cannot use the Service. E and F are optional, and declining them does not prevent use.

Chapter 5 Withdrawal and contact

5.1 Consent may be withdrawn at any time

Withdrawing a required item (A–D) means your child can no longer use Athlomed (the account is closed). Optional items (E, F) may be withdrawn individually.

Nothing detrimental will follow for your child because consent was withdrawn.

Information already viewed at the time of withdrawal cannot be retrospectively unseen.

5.2 Requests for disclosure, correction and deletion

You may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion or suspension of use of your child's records.

Start with the team's trainer, because the personal information handling business operator in respect of your child's records is the trainer who created them. If you cannot reach them, or you would rather contact us directly, use the contact below.

For records that have the character of clinical records, we may respond by suspending access rather than physically deleting them, balancing the need to retain records against the rights of the data subject.

Annex 3: Limits of this method, and implementation notes

This section is for operators. It is not part of the consent text.

3.1 Risk of impersonation

It is the athlete who enters the guardian's email address. This method therefore cannot prevent:

  • an athlete entering another address of their own rather than a guardian's;
  • an athlete entering the address of a friend or other third party.

Email confirmation proves only that the holder of that address consented; it does not prove that that person is the legal representative. This limit cannot be removed technically while this method is used.

3.2 Mitigations (for consideration)

#MeasureEffectCost
1Always include an "if this is unexpected" path in the emailGuardians can detect misdirected or abusive useLow (done in Annex 1)
2Send a copy after consent completesGuardians can notice after the factLow (done in Annex 1)
3Reject a guardian address identical to the athlete's account addressBlocks the simplest self-consentLow
4Require guardian name, relationship and telephone numberFormal deterrentLow
5Let the team's trainer see that guardian consent was completedAllows real-world verification in the fieldMedium
6Allow written (signed) consent as an alternativeHigher certaintyMedium

We recommend implementing at least 1 to 4. Measure 5 has a high return given that trainers usually know the guardians in practice.

3.3 Not yet implemented

#FunctionSection
1A registration flow that detects an age under 18 from the date of birth and requires guardian consentThroughout
2The guardian email field and sending the confirmation email (no email delivery provider is yet contracted)Annex 1
3Link issue, 7-day expiry and single-use enforcement4.3
4Blocking records and connection code issue until guardian consent completes4.2
5Notification to guardians when a connection is formed2.3

Item 2 is a precondition. Until open item 4 in the handover note (no email delivery capability) is resolved, this method cannot operate. Until then, obtain consent on paper or through the team's trainer.
