Preamble

Athlomed is a condition and injury management platform for sports settings.

Athlomed — Sports Medical Management Platform
Connecting the field and medicine: a medical platform for sports settings.
Physical Check / Condition / Injury / Rehabilitation / Return to Play
From physical checks through condition, injury, rehabilitation and return to play.
Bringing the records of the field together into one.

Athlomed is built on the principle of handling data that may constitute medical information to the minimum extent necessary, while providing the recording functions that sports settings actually need. That principle is expressed in the following provisions of these Terms:

  • The athlete decides who may see their records (Articles 14 and 15)
  • Coaches receive neither diagnoses nor treatment details — only the permitted scope of participation and availability (Article 18)
  • Clinical note bodies are not displayed even to the athlete (Article 19)
  • We do not access records beyond what operating the service requires (Article 25)

Article 3 (Nature of the Service)

  1. The Service is not a medical institution and is not intended for the diagnosis, treatment or prevention of disease. The Service is not intended to constitute medical device software under Japan's Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Products Including Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices.
  2. Alerts, ACWR (acute:chronic workload ratio), dehydration rate, inferred participation status and other figures shown by the Service are information that supports judgement in the field, not medical judgement itself. Final judgement must be made by a qualified person on the basis of in-person assessment.
  3. We are not in a position to judge, and do not warrant, the medical accuracy, appropriateness or completeness of anything recorded in the Service.

Article 4 (Composition of the Service)

The Service comprises the following functions. Details and scope of provision are as displayed within the Service.

  • Physical check (measurement template management, team-wide batch entry, alerts on left-right difference and decline from previous values)
  • Athlete basic information (name, date of birth, physical information, dominant hand and foot, sporting history, etc.)
  • Condition (multiple daily condition entries, recording of the location of pain or discomfort, trend visualisation, threshold-based alerts)
  • Practice and match records (pre- and post-activity checks, RPE, change in body weight) and strength training and rehabilitation records (exercises, sets, repetitions, templates)
  • Injury management (five-phase injury board, participation allowances and their history)
  • Rehabilitation and return to play (return phases, return records)
  • Training load (management of session plans and what was carried out, RPE collection, sRPE, ACWR)
  • Clinical notes (SOAP-format records, emergency response records, templates of selectable items)
  • Report output (on-screen, PDF, CSV)
  • Japanese / English display

Chapter 4 Connections

Article 14 (Connecting Athletes and Teams)

  1. A connection between an athlete and a Team is formed by the athlete issuing a Connection Code and a Contracting User receiving it. A Contracting User cannot register an athlete without that athlete's consent.
  2. Connection Codes have the following properties:
    • six digits, displayed once at issue. We store only a hashed value, never the code itself;
    • an expiry selected by the athlete at issue (30 minutes or 24 hours), judged against server time;
    • single use, as a rule;
    • revocable by the athlete at any time before use.
  3. The Team to be connected is determined by the Team into which the Contracting User entering the code is currently logged in.
  4. Where a connection is formed, we notify the athlete. No connection is formed without the athlete's knowledge.
  5. Formation and release of connections are recorded in the audit log.
  6. Issue of a Connection Code requires the consent set out in the Athlete Consent athlete_consent. We record the display language and the version of the consent text at the moment consent was given.

Article 15 (History Scope)

  1. On issuing a Connection Code, the athlete selects the history scope for that Team from the following three options.
History scopeRecords disclosed
From the join date only (default)Only records made on or after the date the connection was formed
Including injury historyThe above, plus past injury records and return records (excluding clinical note bodies)

  1. The athlete may change the history scope at any time after connection. Where the scope is narrowed, the affected records cease to be displayed to that Team's staff immediately.
  2. The athlete may release the connection at any time. The Team Owner may likewise remove an athlete from the Team. In either case, the other party is notified.
  3. Releasing a connection does not delete records. They remain attached to the Athlete Profile and remain visible to the athlete. On reconnection, they are displayed according to the history scope then in force.
  4. After release, clinical notes created by a Contracting User remain under that Contracting User's authority (Article 21). Requests for deletion from the athlete are handled under Article 22.

Article 16 (Connections Between Staff)

  1. A Team Owner may issue a Staff Connection Password to admit another Medical or Coach user to the same Team.
  2. On issue, the Team Owner specifies the role granted, the permission level (edit / view-only), the expiry (1 hour / 24 hours / 7 days) and the maximum number of uses (1 / 3 / unlimited).
  3. The password is displayed once at issue. We store only a hashed value and verify it server-side.
  4. Management of Staff Connection Passwords is the responsibility of the Team Owner who issued them. If a password reaches a third party, an unrelated person may gain access to athletes' health information. Set expiry to the minimum necessary.
  5. A person without a valid contract cannot join a Team as a Contracting User. Where a contract lapses after joining, only viewing and export remain available (Article 11).
  6. A Team Owner may remove participating staff at any time, with immediate effect.

Article 17 (Team Switching and Re-authentication)

  1. Where a User belonging to several Teams switches Team, we require the password to be entered again.
  2. The Service displays the data of only one Team at a time. This prevents recording against the wrong Team, and prevents successive viewing of several Teams' data when a device is left unattended.
  3. A Team session expires eight hours after the last operation.
  4. Team switches are recorded in the audit log.

Chapter 5 Handling of Information

Article 18 (Scope of Information Provided to Coaches)

  1. We do not display the following to Users in the Coach role:
    • diagnoses;
    • injury mechanism and whether surgery was performed;
    • clinical note bodies, findings and emergency response records;
    • measurement notes;
    • items of the athlete's basic information other than name, jersey number and position.
  2. Coaches receive the participation allowance expressly written by a Medical user (allowance level, permitted actions, prohibited actions, time and intensity limits, validity date), the injured body part, the rehabilitation phase, the day's status and sport participation decision set by the Medical user in the clinical notes, trends in condition and in practice and match records (within the history scope), measured values, and availability. Where the participation allowance and the clinical notes differ as to the day's status, the more restrictive of the two is displayed.
  3. Coaches may create and edit the Team's sessions (plans for practices and matches and what was carried out).
  4. Permitted and prohibited actions are stored as predefined keys and displayed in the viewer's language. This separates the information whose misreading carries the greatest cost from the uncertainty of translation. Free-text remarks are displayed in the language in which they were entered.
  5. A participation allowance past its validity date is displayed as "requires re-assessment", with its contents withheld. Expired entries are also excluded from automatic inference of availability. Medical users are responsible for setting validity dates and re-assessing within them.
  6. Coaches must not use athlete information obtained through the Service beyond what the Team's activities require, and must not disclose it to third parties without the athlete's consent.

Article 19 (Information Not Displayed to Athletes)

  1. On the Service's screens, athletes may view the records they entered themselves (basic information, condition records, practice and match records, strength training and rehabilitation records, RPE), their own measurement records (measured values), and the plans and content of sessions in which they are included.
  2. Athletes cannot view clinical note (SOAP) bodies (including emergency response records). This avoids the misunderstanding and anxiety caused by reading provisional impressions and differential reasoning, and avoids the deterioration in the quality of clinical records that occurs when they are written for the subject to read.
  3. Injury records, participation allowances, return records and measurement notes are not displayed to athletes on the Service's screens. Athletes receive explanations of their contents from their Team's Medical users.
  4. Notwithstanding the preceding two paragraphs, athletes may make a request under Article 22.

Article 20 (Accuracy of Entered Information)

  1. Users shall endeavour to ensure that the information they enter is accurate.
  2. Contracting Users are responsible for confirming that valid consent exists from the athlete before recording information about them. The consent screens we provide support that confirmation; they do not warrant that consent was validly obtained in the field.
  3. Athletes may delete condition records they entered in error. Correction is effected by adding a new record.
  4. Athletes may edit their own basic information at any time, except that the date of birth, which is used to determine whether guardian consent is required, cannot be changed from the athlete’s own screen after registration. To change it, contact us at the point of contact in Article 30.

  3. Where the Contracting User cannot be reached, or where the data subject prefers to contact us directly, we receive the request at the contact point in Article 30 and refer it to the Contracting User as necessary.
