About this form

This form covers one disclosure of information to someone outside Athlomed.

Trainers and coaches of a connected team viewing records inside Athlomed is not covered here — that is covered by Part B of the Athlete Consent. This form is needed when information recorded in Athlomed goes to a person or organisation outside Athlomed.

CoveredNot covered
Sending the course of an injury to a hospital being visitedA connected team's trainer viewing records

Sending records to the medical staff of a national or selection squadAn athlete exporting their own data
Submitting injury records to an insurerA trainer exporting data within their own authority
Sending records to a new school or club other than by connection—

One form per disclosure. If the recipient changes, or the content changes, consent is sought again.

Chapter 2 Points to confirm

2.1 This consent covers the one disclosure described above

Consent will be sought again for a different recipient or different content. Signing this form does not authorise future disclosures.

2.2 You may decline

No detriment will follow from declining. It will not affect playing time, selection or how you are treated within the team.

2.3 You may withdraw at any time

If the disclosure has not yet been made, withdrawal prevents it.

Information already disclosed cannot be recovered from the recipient. Please be aware of this.

2.4 Handling by the recipient

How the recipient handles the information is governed by that recipient's own rules. Neither Athlomed nor the trainer making the disclosure is responsible for how it is handled afterwards.

Where the recipient is a medical institution, that institution is subject to confidentiality obligations under medical legislation.

2.5 A record of the disclosure is created

A record is created and retained of the recipient, the categories of information disclosed, the purpose, the date and time, and the person making the disclosure. This is required by law. The athlete and guardian may request disclosure of that record.

2.6 Athlomed is not a medical institution

The information disclosed does not replace a doctor's diagnosis or judgement. What is recorded in Athlomed is a record made in a sports setting, not a medical chart.

2.7 Where clinical note bodies or photographs are included

Where clinical note (SOAP) bodies and findings or photographs of the affected area are to be disclosed under Chapter 1, the trainer making the disclosure must explain their content to the athlete (and guardian) before obtaining consent.

Athletes cannot normally view clinical note bodies in the Service (Athlete Consent A-6). An explanation is therefore mandatory here, so that nothing goes outside without the athlete knowing what it contains.

2.8 Purposes for which disclosure must not be made

Disclosure for the following purposes is outside the scope of this form and must not be made (Terms of Service, Article 23(6)):

  • for use in selection, employment, career placement or the terms of a contract (unless the athlete themselves requests it and this is stated in the "purpose" field in Chapter 1);
  • for press, publicity or social media;
  • for use in deciding whether, or on what terms, to underwrite insurance (unless the athlete themselves requests it, for example to make a claim).

2.9 Athletes under 18

For athletes under 18, the consent of a guardian (legal representative) is required. We apply a stricter standard than the statutory benchmark and cover everyone under 18.

2.10 Disclosure abroad

Where the recipient is outside Japan, that fact and an outline of the personal information protection regime of the recipient's country will be explained before disclosure. Always enter the country name in the "recipient address" field in Chapter 1.

Annex: For the trainer making the disclosure

The party making a disclosure under this form is you, the Contracting User — not Athlomed. You are the personal information handling business operator in respect of the record, and the responsibility for the lawfulness of the disclosure is yours (Privacy Policy, Article 3).

Before disclosing, confirm:

  • [ ] every field in Chapter 1 is completed specifically (no blanks, no "etc.")
  • [ ] the scope disclosed is the minimum necessary for the purpose
  • [ ] where clinical note bodies or photographs are included, you have explained their content to the athlete (and guardian)
  • [ ] where the athlete is under 18, you have the legal representative's consent
  • [ ] where the recipient is abroad, you have explained the country and its regime
  • [ ] the purpose is not one prohibited under 2.8
  • [ ] you have created and retained the record of the disclosure (recipient, categories, purpose, date and time, person disclosing)
  • [ ] where you exported data from the Service, you have confirmed the export is recorded in the audit log
Note on implementation: Generating this form within the Service and recording consent there is not implemented as at the date of this document. Until then, use this document as a paper or PDF form. Exports from the Service made for a disclosure are recorded in the audit log.
